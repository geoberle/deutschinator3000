# ADHD-Friendly Improvements — Backlog

Improvements identified from ADHD learning research review. Ordered by impact.

## Streak tracking + session persistence
Track practice streaks in localStorage. Show "3 Tage in Folge!" on home screen.
Track per-set best scores. Show "Neuer Rekord!" when beaten.
Biggest lever for **return motivation** — ADHD struggles most with coming back, not starting.

## Bold key patterns in explanations
Explanations after wrong answers are text-heavy. ADHD kids may not read them.
Bold the key rule ("werden + Partizip II = Vorgangspassiv") and visually de-emphasize the rest.
Could use a `<mark>` or distinct styling for the pattern line vs. the prose.

## "Recommended next" on home screen
10 exercise sets with no guidance = choice paralysis.
Add "Empfohlen" section — surface sets with worst past scores or never-attempted.
One prominent "Weiter üben" button that auto-picks the next thing.

## Spaced repetition across sessions
Wrong answers tracked only within one session. No cross-session review.
Track wrong-answer patterns in localStorage. Surface "Wiederholung" set on home
with exercises previously answered wrong. Highest long-term learning impact.

## Relax challenge lock-out
Locked stages block progress if kid gets stuck. ADHD + frustration = abandonment.
Allow skipping after attempt (even if failed), or unlock next stage after *attempting*
all sets in current stage rather than requiring all correct.

## Optional speed mode
"Schnellrunde" — show time per question, track best times. Gives novelty to repeated
practice. Strictly optional, never forced. Some ADHD kids thrive with gentle self-competition.
